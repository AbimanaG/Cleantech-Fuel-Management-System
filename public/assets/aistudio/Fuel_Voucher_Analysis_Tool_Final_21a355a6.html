FUEL VOUCHER ANALYSIS TOOL

1. Upload report

The PDF is read entirely in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere. Once a row is loaded, its Usage Date, Vehicle Number, Liters, Fuel Type and Price are locked. The report's single mileage value is placed in Meter Reading End (a mileage of 3 means the vehicle has no mileage, so it is left blank). You can add the Meter Reading Start, Area and the split lines of Common - Petrol/Diesel rows.

2. Vehicle → Area list

This list gives each vehicle its suggested Area (used by ✓ Tick all suggested areas). It is saved in this browser. Download it to edit in Excel or keep a backup, then Upload it back — or load it on another computer. Columns: Vehicle Number and Area (one row per vehicle; add a second row for a second area — the first row is the suggestion).

3. Pending transactions — confirm Area, then select

Selection rule: a transaction cannot be selected until its Area is entered and confirmed with ✓ OK. Click ✓ Tick all suggested areas to auto-fill and confirm every row that has a known area suggestion. For a Common - Diesel/Petrol card, click Split usage — the split total must match the pumped liters exactly. Once rows are ticked, the bottom bar appears — enter the Voucher No (fuel type and price come from the report), then apply.
